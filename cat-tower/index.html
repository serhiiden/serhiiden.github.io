About

A black cat wakes up in a quiet yard and starts walking. He has no goal — only the road. Somewhere at the edge of the world stands the Tower: a giant scratching post.

On the way: rooftops and stairwells, a railway station, flooded streets, tunnels, a desert, mountains, a field of bones and the sea. A kitten joins the journey. A Black Cat with red eyes keeps appearing ahead — watching, meddling, vanishing.

Cat Tower is a wordless, atmospheric puzzle-platformer. There is no text in the game at all. Everything you see is drawn procedurally in code, and every sound is synthesized.

  • Push crates, carry lanterns and keys, ride rafts and a handcar, climb fences and ropes
  • Sneak past sleeping dogs, outrun rats, read the tide and the wind
  • Look after the kitten — it follows you and sometimes helps
  • Checkpoints everywhere; the game saves on its own
