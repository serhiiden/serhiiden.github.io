About

Build your own 3D printer from scratch, and find out why your prints come out the way they do.

Pick the frame, motors, belts, hotend and board, drive in screws of the right length, tension the belts, plug every wire into its port, level the bed and run the self-test. Then print, and watch every choice show up in the model: layer shifts from a slack belt, ripples after corners from a wobbly frame, elephant's foot from a nozzle set too low, stringing from a Bowden extruder. After each print the game names every defect and points at the part to blame.

What's inside

  • Four kinds of printer: Cartesian, CoreXY, Delta and a cantilever
  • 50 orders with budgets and requirements — quiet, fast, precise, strong, the right plastic — and a new order every day
  • Repairs: a client brings a broken printer; find the fault from a test print and fix it
  • A service career: fix clients' printers for pay and experience, learn skills that make your hands faster, restore flea-market printers and sell them
  • Dozens of parts with real trade-offs, upgrades, PLA, PETG, ABS and TPU
  • A slicer and firmware: layer height, infill, retraction, supports, e-steps, PID, acceleration, Linear Advance
  • The workshop: your own print farm with a job queue, deadlines and a reputation
  • A free mode — print your own STL models too

No internet needed. Music and sounds are synthesised right in the game.
